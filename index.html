    What I’m doing now

    Test Infrastructure Engineer

    purpl, Purdue’s undergraduate rocket and propulsion lab · since March 2026

    I build the electronics that let the propulsion test stand measure accurately. That includes a board that converts industrial 4–20 mA sensor signals to 0–10 V for the data system, and a two-layer load cell board whose readout cancels out supply drift. I’m also planning the data acquisition setup for 20–30 analog inputs and 15 thermocouples.

    Undergraduate Researcher

    TRACE Lab, humanoid robotics · since October 2026

    I’m helping a Booster T1 humanoid robot learn to play table tennis. The work uses reinforcement learning in Python and C++ on the PACE framework, plus collecting and analyzing the experimental data.

    Computer Science Teaching Assistant

    Purdue Department of Computer Science · since January 2026

    I help hundreds of students with programming concepts and debugging in office hours, and I run a weekly lab of 30 students working through hands-on C exercises.

    SMART P.I.G pipeline robot

    A robot that crawls through pipes, designed and built from scratch: the body in Siemens NX CAD and the control logic on an ESP32. I also programmed maze-solving and obstacle-course robots that find their own way using magnetic and infrared sensors.
